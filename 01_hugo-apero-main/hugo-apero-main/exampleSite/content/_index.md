  Born and raised in Tegucigalpa, Honduras, I am interested in how development in the periphery is actually lived and how rigorous evidence suggests it may be improved. My interests include randomized controlled trials (RCTs), Liberation Theology, and the impacts of crime in Latin America. I am also interested in what rigorous econometrics and a rigorous ethnographic sensibility might create when brought together. In the short term, I am working to contribute to evidence-based development work in Central America through J-PAL LAC’s office in Guatemala; in the longer term, I hope to continue this work through graduate study.
